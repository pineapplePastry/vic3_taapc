# Overview
**Tank and Aeroplane Production Controls** is a Victoria 3 mod that allows for a little more granularity when deciding how much automobile production should be sacrificed for tank and/or aeroplane production.

[Steam Page](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3818927427)

## Content
This mod adjusts the production methods of the Automotive Industries building, with three new methods added.

For aeroplanes:
- No Aeroplane Production
- Aeroplane Production - produces 5 Aeroplanes at the cost of 2 Fabric and Hardwood, instead of 5 Automobiles (50% of inputs and outputs).
- Mass Aeroplane Production **(NEW)** - The base production method, producing 10 Aeroplanes at the cost of 4 Fabric and Hardwood, instead of 10 Automobiles.

For tanks:
- No Tank Production
- Tank Production - produces 4 Tanks at the cost of 1 Steel, instead of 4 Automobiles (20% of inputs and outputs).
- Increased Tank Production **(NEW)** - produces 12 Tanks at the cost of 3 Steel, instead of 12 Automobiles (60% of inputs and outputs).
- Mass Tank Production **(NEW)**  - The base production method, producing 20 Tanks at the cost of 5 Steel, instead of 20 Automobiles.

Additional workforce from the new production methods is also scaled accordingly. Further divisions were not made since it would have resulted in non-whole number workforces.
